WorkMate Account Deletion

WorkMate (package: com.workmate.natore) provides users with a way to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your WorkMate account.
  2. Use the subject: WorkMate Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your WorkMate account email address and, if applicable, your registered phone number.
  4. We will verify the request and process the account and associated data deletion.
Request Account Deletion

What will be deleted

After a verified deletion request is processed, WorkMate will delete the account and associated user data that is maintained by WorkMate, subject to any data that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory reasons.

Processing

Deletion requests are reviewed and processed within a reasonable period. If additional verification is needed to protect the account, we may request information necessary to verify ownership.
