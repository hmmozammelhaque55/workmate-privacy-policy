Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

WorkMate ("WorkMate", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile application that helps users discover and publish jobs, worker profiles, bed-space/house-rent listings, buy-and-sell listings, and service-related content.

This Privacy Policy explains what information WorkMate may collect, how we use it, how it may be shared, how we protect it, and how users can request deletion of their account and associated data.

1. Information We Collect

Account and authentication information

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address, when provided through registration or Google sign-in.
  • Phone number and WhatsApp number, when provided.
  • Authentication identifiers required to maintain the user account.
  • Profile photo, when the user chooses to upload one.

Profile and listing information

Depending on the features a user chooses to use, WorkMate may store information entered by the user in worker profiles, supplier profiles, job posts, bed-space/house-rent listings, buy-and-sell listings, and other service-related posts. This can include descriptions, categories, employment-related information, prices, salary information, locations, contact information, photos, and other information the user voluntarily submits.

Comments and interactions

WorkMate may store comments, replies, views, likes, dislikes, saved items, ratings, notifications, and other interactions associated with the application.

Device and technical information

WorkMate and its service providers may process technical information needed to operate the application, such as device/app information, identifiers, notification tokens, crash or diagnostic information, and network-related information. Some information may be processed by third-party services used by the application.

Advertising information

WorkMate displays banner advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising services may process information according to their own policies for delivering, measuring, and improving advertising.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain user accounts.
  • To authenticate users and provide account-related features.
  • To display profiles, posts, listings, comments, and other content that users choose to publish.
  • To allow users to contact or communicate with other users when the relevant feature is used.
  • To send relevant notifications, including comment and account-related notifications.
  • To provide search, category, saved-item, profile, and marketplace functionality.
  • To maintain application security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • To display and measure banner advertising.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell users' personal information as a standalone product. Information may be shared or made available in the following circumstances:

  • With other WorkMate users: information that a user intentionally publishes in a public profile, post, listing, comment, or contact section may be visible to other users as part of the requested application functionality.
  • With service providers: WorkMate uses services such as Firebase/Google services for authentication, database, storage, notifications, hosting or related application operations, and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for banner advertising.
  • For legal or safety reasons: information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, enforce rights, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect users, the public, or the service.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data transmitted between the app and supported services may use encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and other information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain legitimate business and security functions, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. When an account deletion request is completed, we delete the user's account and associated data that we are required and able to delete, subject to any data that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other lawful purposes.

6. Account and Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their WorkMate account and associated data through the account-deletion feature provided in the app. Users may also use the external account-deletion request method provided by WorkMate where available.

When an account deletion request is processed, associated account data and user-created data are deleted from our systems where applicable, subject to legitimate retention requirements described above.

7. Children's Privacy

WorkMate is not directed to children under the applicable minimum age for using the service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

8. Third-Party Services

WorkMate uses third-party services, including Google Firebase services and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). These providers may process information in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies. Users should review the applicable third-party policies for additional information about their processing practices.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this same web address with a revised effective date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions, data requests, or account-deletion assistance, please contact the WorkMate support/privacy contact listed on the WorkMate Google Play listing.
